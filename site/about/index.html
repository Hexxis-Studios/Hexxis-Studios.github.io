A studio built
on curiosity.

I’m Daymien Vanhorn, founder of Hexxis Studios.

I build software, games, and practical tools, drawing on web development and a decade of hands-on PC building and troubleshooting—more than 100 builds and rebuilds.

My work includes HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and custom WordPress themes, with Unity and Blender for game and 3D projects. I also direct AI-assisted development from requirements and iteration through testing and refinement.

From a website for a local business to writing software and desktop utilities, I focus on useful tools, clear interfaces, thoughtful performance, and user privacy.
