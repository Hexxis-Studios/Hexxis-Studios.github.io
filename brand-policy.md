That permission does not authorize you to operate as Hexxis Studios, use its distinctive logos as your own, redistribute its reserved creative materials, or imply an official relationship. The name identifies Daymien Vanhorn's studio; this policy does not assert that it is a registered trademark.

Before publishing a derivative:

- Replace the studio name, wordmark, logo artwork, branded emblem, and custom illustrated icons with your own assets.
- Replace portraits, biographical text, project copy, thumbnails, screenshots, and other reserved content with material you have permission to use.
- Replace contact addresses, account links, social metadata, canonical URLs, favicons, and structured identity data. Do not leave users thinking they are contacting the original studio.
- Remove the original analytics endpoint and any studio-specific service configuration. Use your own services and an accurate privacy notice.
- Preserve required code-license and third-party notices, without presenting those notices as endorsement.

## Examples

Allowed: a distinctly rebranded website using the layout, filters, motion techniques, or rendering code; factual plain-text attribution such as "Website code adapted from Hexxis Studios"; linking to the official site; and truthful reviews or other uses permitted by law.

Not authorized by the code license: publishing the original branded site as your own; an account or store using the studio's identity to appear official; selling "official Hexxis Studios" services without permission; or using its logo, portrait, or project artwork as the branding for your business. Merely adding credit does not grant permission to reuse reserved assets.

Ordinary icosahedron geometry, generic interface concepts, and independently made designs are not reserved by this policy. It does not prohibit lawful nominative reference, fair use, or other statutory exceptions. No additional restrictions are imposed on the MIT-licensed code itself.
